Activity log: design ideas. Every change is recorded as one entry: who (you, a store sync, a plugin, Questkeep itself), a one-line summary of why, and expandable details of what (field, before → after). Changes made together (one sync, one bulk edit) form a batch, so a Steam sync is one line in the global log that expands to the games it touched. Click the tabs to switch views; click entries to expand them.
