IGDB matching picks the right game more often: when several IGDB games share a name, the automatic lookup prefers the PC version over consoles, phones and ports. The search for linking a game shows platforms and developer next to the year ("Game Boy Advance port · Pocket Studios"), PC games first. Games from a store can now be pointed at the right IGDB game too (Edit > Metadata). Cover and background searches on SteamGridDB also use the release year, so same-named games (like the two Need for Speed: Most Wanted) get their own artwork.
